How the setting was chosen
  Selection used the TRAINING tests only (T02-T08): the default W_NEG=2,
  purity 0.75 is the only setting that beats reactive CG+PG on both axes
  there (fewer extra cycles AND more power-off domain-cycles), for both
  grains. The held-out columns (T09-T11, T14-T16; T12/T13 are forced tests)
  were not used to choose; they show the generalisation gap: the models
  then trade power-off time for fewer wake stalls instead of improving both.
  Retrain on benchmark traces (ml/run_ml_train.sh) before quoting ML results.
Columns: extra cycles = DUT - REF summed (stall cost, % of REF cycles);
  PG-off domain-cycles = sum over power domains of cycles not ON;
  TP/FP/FN = idle periods gated+long / gated+short / not gated+long
  (long = idle >= 24 cycles).
